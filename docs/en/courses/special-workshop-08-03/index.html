Empowerment Through Connection: A Special 08/03 Celebration

A confident and empowered woman is bold, insightful, and impactful. She expresses herself with grace, articulates her emotions thoughtfully, and presents her ideas with both logic and assurance. Her presence captivates, her words resonate, and she inspires those around her to connect on a deeper level.

This 08/03, we invite you to a workshop that celebrates and nurtures these qualities. For women, it's about embracing your voice and owning your influence with confidence. For men, it’s a chance to understand and support the incredible women in your life, fostering stronger, more authentic connections. Together, let’s build a team that thrives on mutual respect, clear communication, and shared empowerment.

Curious to see how this workshop can transform your team dynamics? Explore what’s possible.

Why should you choose this event to celebrate 08/03?

Bridge the Gender Communication Gap

Gain insights into effective strategies for men to empathize with and support women in both personal and professional contexts.

Enhance Collaboration Between Gender

Develop skills to foster collaboration and mutual respect, enhancing teamwork and relationships in diverse environments.

Empower Your Emotional Intelligence

Discover how to leverage Emotional Intelligence to deepen connections and improve communication between men and women.

Foster Effective Communication for Women

Learn techniques for women to articulate their desires and needs confidently, ensuring their voices are heard without being perceived as overly emotional.

This workshop is specially designed:

  • For Women Leaders: Confidently express your needs and ideas with clarity and persuasion without being perceived as overly emotional. Strengthen your leadership presence by ensuring your voice is heard and your influence is recognized within the organization.
  • For Men Leaders: Develop the skills to understand and support female colleagues, fostering effective collaboration and promoting a fair, respectful workplace. Learn to recognize emotional nuances to communicate and lead your team more effectively.
  • For Teams: Build a cohesive, open, and high-performing work environment through effective communication and mutual understanding.
  • For Personal & Leadership Growth: Apply Emotional Intelligence to enhance connection, build strong relationships, and expand your influence within the organization.

About The New Leaders

The New Leaders is the leading organization to provide emotional intelligence (EQ) leadership training with the frameworks from worldwide accredited leadership programs of Harvard Kennedy School, Cornell University, and Oxford University.

The New Leaders' programs are designed with scenario-based approach based on real business situations, highly practical and applicable, along with post-training programs to create lasting long-term impacts that are measurable for businesses.

With a mission to advance EQ Leadership & Communication skills to a world-class standard for leaders and their team members, The New Leaders always strives for improvement in training approaches and tries to find best solutions to help businesses save costs and maximize human performance, creating a positive working environment and being ready for innovation and breakthroughs.
