Every Tết, we ask ourselves: what gift is elegant enough, meaningful enough, and worthy enough for the people who have walked alongside us?

And yet we still reach for cakes, tea, and wine - sweet gifts that fade quickly. After the lively days of spring, all that remains is an empty box and a wish that drifts away. Meanwhile, what teams truly need for the year ahead - knowledge, motivation, and belief in themselves - is rarely delivered the right way. The New Leaders believes the most valuable Tết gift lies not in material things, but in the power to inspire and to walk with the recipient throughout the year.

From that wish, the Tết Gift Box was born - shaped around a handwritten letter, a notebook, a Fortune Cookie, a Motivation Card... as a reminder that: “Thank you for your effort. Thank you for being by our side.” This is more than a gift. It is a moment that transfers energy, helping the recipient feel valued, uplifted, and inspired with positive leadership to begin a new journey. A small gift - yet with the power to create great change.  A Tết warm enough - and deep enough to start a breakthrough year.

About The New Leaders

The New Leaders is a company that does more than train EQ - we build a complete leadership development journey grounded in emotional intelligence. For us, EQ is not a single skill, but the foundation for every leadership capability: strategic thinking, communication, feedback, coaching, conflict management, and building team culture. Each of our programs is designed as a tightly connected system of competencies, helping leaders and teams not only learn, but truly apply and change in their daily work. The New Leaders walks alongside every level of the organization because we believe: when each person grows in EQ, the whole organization becomes stronger and more resilient.
