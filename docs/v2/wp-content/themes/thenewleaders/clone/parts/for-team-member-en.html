EQ Communication programs for team members

Our customized training program is designed with scenario-based approach, interactive activities and discussions. The training is designed based on the leadership framework from Harvard Kennedy School and Oxford University.

Self-awareness

Help individuals to be more aware of themselves on a deeper level as they will explore about:

  • Internal self-awareness: help individuals to be more proactive and responsible.
  • External self-awareness: understand how to act, react and behave in the right manner.

Understanding Others' Emotions and Behaviors

Effectively connect with one another by learning the core EQ communication skills to understand others’ emotions and behaviors.

This leads to the outcome of fostering smooth communication, mutual support, promoting a positive working environment, and enhancing team productivity.

Giving effective feedback

Provide individuals the knowledge to give opinions that others, be it subordinates, colleagues or managers, want to listen and maintain harmony in relationships.

Conflict Management

Provide individuals the knowledge to communicate better in tense situations through:

  • The right mindset when facing conflicts: not all conflicts are unhealthy!
  • Persuasive communication: protect their points and convince others.
  • Dealing with difficult people: ease the tension in the conversation with the right languages (words, body gestures, etc).

Presentation and Ideas Discussion

Improve your team discussions and meetings' productivity through:

  • Persuasive presentation: understand how to generate a clear and effective presentation
  • Handling meaningful discussion: maintain the atmosphere and facilitate constructive discussion.
