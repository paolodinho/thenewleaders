Will introverts have fewer opportunities?

In today's business world, introverts are often undervalued. A 2009 study in Industrial and Organizational Psychology in the US showed that only 4% of managers and directors are introverts, with this percentage decreasing at higher levels, contrasting with the 50% of introverts in the general population.

Does this mean introverts have no chance? The truth is, we possess unique advantages - the key to becoming outstanding leaders. From deep listening skills to strategic thinking, our potential is limitless.

It's time to harness the unique power of introverts in leadership roles. Join now to unlock your potential and boost your career through EQ!
