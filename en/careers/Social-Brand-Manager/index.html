The New Leaders

Social & Brand Manager

  • Open for recruitment
  • Full-time

About The New Leaders

The New Leaders is a leadership training and development company focused on Emotional Intelligence, neuroscience, and modern leadership thinking. We partner with businesses, corporations, and leadership communities to build leadership capability rooted in human connection, emotional awareness, and positive influence. As we step into 2026, The New Leaders is entering a strong growth phase—expanding our brand, partner ecosystem, and social/brand platform to bring modern leadership knowledge to more generations of leaders. We’re looking for a Social & Brand Manager to shape the brand for our next stage of growth.

Your Role

You will play a strategic role in:

  • Shaping the long-term brand direction
  • Building a social & brand ecosystem aligned with business growth
  • Gradually building and growing the Social & Brand team during our scale-up journey

Key Responsibilities

1. Social & Brand Strategy

  • Develop and execute the brand strategy for The New Leaders
  • Build a cohesive, multi-platform brand identity across social channels

2. Content & Community Ecosystem

  • Develop a content strategy around leadership, EQ, and neuroscience that is inspiring and deep
  • Translate expert knowledge into accessible, emotionally resonant, high-impact content
  • Build and grow a community centered on leadership and human development

3. Partnerships & Brand Collaboration

  • Develop brand collaboration strategies with local and international partners
  • Work with educational institutions, leadership networks, and professional communities
  • Partner with the leadership team to expand our ecosystem in line with brand strategy

4. Events, Workshops & Brand Experience

  • Propose and deliver creative event/workshop strategies
  • Design brand experiences that bring The New Leaders’ knowledge and values closer to the community
  • Blend social, offline events, and storytelling to create strong emotional brand impact

5. Branding & Marketing Materials

  • Develop and manage brand visuals, identity system, and marketing materials
  • Ensure all assets consistently reflect The New Leaders’ spirit and standards

6. Build & Grow the Social & Brand Team

  • Gradually build and scale the Social & Brand team
  • Coach and develop team capability throughout the scale-up phase

Who We’re Looking For

You may be a great fit if you:

  • Have 5+ years of experience in Social, Branding, or Marketing
  • Are passionate about education, leadership, emotional intelligence, and human development
  • Know how to connect brand with emotion, values, and real people
  • Are highly creative, modern in thinking, and not tied to traditional “education branding” templates
  • Are proactive, accountable, growth-minded, and committed to continuous learning
  • Enjoy fast-growing environments where your work genuinely shapes strategy

Benefits & Growth Opportunities

  • A people-first, open, and energetic working environment
  • Deep exposure and training in Leadership, Emotional Intelligence, and Neuroscience
  • Strong career growth alongside the rapid expansion of The New Leaders
  • Space to create, experiment, and build new brand models
  • Work closely with experts and local/international partners
  • Competitive compensation, aligned with your capability and contribution

Why This Role Is Special

This isn’t just a branding role. You’ll be building a leadership brand that can influence generations.

If you believe branding is about emotion, values, and long-term impact, we’d love to connect and explore the journey together.
