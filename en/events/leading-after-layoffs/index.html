Layoffs don't just cut costs — they cut deep into an organization's trust, culture, and internal strength.

After each layoff, team performance drops by an average of 20% within six months (Culture Amp, 2024), while employee trust in the company falls from 80% to 63% (HBR, 2024). It can take 18–24 months for organizational morale to recover — if it ever does.

Layoffs can be a strategic move. But it’s the way organizations communicate, operate, and lead afterward that determines whether teams will burn out or rise stronger.

Leading After Layoffs equips leaders with refined communication strategies to preserve culture, rebuild trust, and drive rapid organizational recovery.

Join our event to retain your team, rebuild trust, and secure your future!

This event is for you if:

  • You need a solution to sustain team performance and motivation immediately after layoffs, instead of letting your organization slip into disarray and loss of direction.
  • You want to communicate layoffs with empathy and precision — preserving internal trust and building a sustainable foundation for recovery.
  • You are seeking a practical and actionable approach to reignite motivation, strengthen team cohesion, and protect your organizational culture during times of disruption.
  • You recognize that your ability to maintain trust and stabilize the team immediately after disruption will determine whether your organization recovers quickly or sinks deeper into prolonged decline.
