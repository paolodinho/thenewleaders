Learn more about EQ in the way you communicate!

Listen with intention:

When you are the one asking, practice deep listening skills. Listen to empathize, to understand, without any judgment or prejudice.

Show that you care:

Ask follow-up questions to gain deeper understanding. For example, ask them to describe a specific situation in detail, inquire about their thoughts or feelings at that time, and ask why? (why did they make that choice? why did they feel that way?…). These questions also help them understand themselves better.

Be genuine, be open:

Then it's your turn, share authentically. Let your emotions flow and thoughts unfold. This transparency deepens our mutual understanding and brings us closer.
