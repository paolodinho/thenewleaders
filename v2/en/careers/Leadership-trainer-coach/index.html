Leadership Trainer/Coach/Speaker (C-Level or Senior Executive)

  • Open for recruitment
  • Contractor

At The New Leaders, we are on a mission to build a new generation of conscious, emotionally intelligent, and future-ready leaders. Our programs are trusted by leading organizations across Vietnam and the region, including JW Marriott, MUFG, Vietcombank, RMIT, VNG, Heineken, the Obama Foundation, and more. We design and deliver leadership development programs rooted in emotional intelligence, strategic thinking, and human connection. Our work spans industries and organizational levels — and we’re now looking for experienced C-level leaders or senior executives to join us in shaping the next wave of leadership.

🚀 Your Role As a Leadership Program Trainer/Coach, you will: Co-create & co-deliver leadership training programs with The New Leaders Share your expertise through speaking engagements, workshops, or coaching sessions Inspire mid- to senior-level leaders through your personal leadership journey and practical experience Collaborate with our Learning Designers & Creative Team to transform your knowledge into engaging, science-backed learning materials Support our goal of creating real impact — not just knowledge transfer, but leadership transformation.

✅ We’re Looking For Leaders Who: Are C-level or senior executives with 15+ years of leadership experience Possess a modern, human-centric view of leadership and organizational growth Are passionate about giving back, mentoring, and developing others

Have experience in one or more of the following areas (or propose your own!): Leadership & People Development Emotional Intelligence & Communication Executive Productivity & Performance Strategy & Change Management Innovation & Entrepreneurship AI in Business Resilience Organizational Culture & Sustainability

(Plus) Have coaching/training certifications, and previous speaking/training experience with proven frameworks. Language: fluent in both Vietnamese and English

💡 What You’ll Get The opportunity to shape future leaders across sectors and industries Collaborate with a creative, mission-driven team of learning designers and facilitators Turn your legacy, stories, and insights into powerful learning tools Flexible commitment – tailored to your availability and interest Expand your influence across our regional partner network Income package will be discussed to suitable collaborations.
